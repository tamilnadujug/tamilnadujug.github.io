### Event Overview

Our November community meetup at Thoughtworks, Coimbatore brought together over 80 attendees for a morning of rigorous engineering discussions.

### Sessions Covered

1. **Spring AI – Practical Usage Notes** by Saravana
2. **Writing Better APIs with Java** by Sathish Kumar Thiyagarajan

Both sessions included interactive Q&A where participants discussed production challenges around token rate limits, backward-compatible schema evolutions, and concurrency monitoring.

### Community Impact

We welcomed student delegates from SECE, Kongu, and PSG Tech who connected directly with mentors working at top software consulting and product organizations.

We look forward to hosting our next gathering!
