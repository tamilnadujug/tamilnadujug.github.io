### An Unforgettable Community Road Trip

As part of the Tamil Nadu Java User Group, organizers coordinated special community bus delegations from **Coimbatore, Madurai, and Chennai** to attend JavaFest at the Infosys Campus.

### Community Collaboration in Transit

During the travel, seasoned engineering leaders, startup founders, faculty advisors, and college students came together. The buses became mobile hack spaces where participants shared career journeys, discussed JVM bytecode, and debugged open source code.

### Key Highlights

- **Hands-on Tracks**: Engaging sessions on Java 25 features, virtual threads under high load, and Spring AI.
- **Networking Pods**: Collegiate chapter leads met engineering executives from premier tech firms.
- **Author Signings**: Participants received signed copies of contemporary Java reference guides.

A heartfelt thanks to all organizers, college faculty champions, and student volunteers who made this possible!
