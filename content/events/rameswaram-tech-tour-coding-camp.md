## About the Tech Tour & Coding Camp

The **Rameswaram Tech Tour and Coding Camp** was an immersive community retreat bringing together passionate student builders, volunteers, and mentors for intensive coding, systems design discussions, and community bonding in Rameswaram.

The initiative took developer enablement beyond metropolitan tech hubs, creating focused environments for deep technical collaboration and open-source project development.
