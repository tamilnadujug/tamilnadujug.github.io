## About DevCarnival 2027

**DevCarnival 2027** is a premier community-driven developer conference taking place at the **Infosys Campus in Mahindra City, Chennai, on January 8–9, 2027**. Centered on the theme *"Technology. People. Purpose."*, the conference brings together developers, open-source contributors, technology leaders, and engineering students from across India.

**Tamil Nadu Java User Group (TNJUG)** is an official Community Partner for the conference.

## Keynote Speaker Announcement

We are delighted and proud to announce our distinguished Keynote Speaker:
- **Prof. Kamakoti Veezhinathan** — Director, Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IIT Madras)
- [LinkedIn Profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kamakoti-veezhinathan-720a4414/)

## Conference Scope

- **Tracks**: 8+ parallel technology tracks spanning AI and developer productivity, cloud-native engineering, Java platforms, data architecture, and emerging systems.
- **Sessions**: 50+ technical talks and practical workshops.
- **Technology Pavilion**: 100+ interactive project demonstrations and exhibits.

## Confirmed Sessions

### 1. Keynote Address
- **Speaker**: Prof. Kamakoti Veezhinathan (Director, IIT Madras)
- **Theme**: Future of computing architectures, indigenization of high-performance tech, and technology for societal purpose.

### 2. The AI Native Developer: What Programming Languages Will Matter Tomorrow?
- **Speaker**: Samir Kamerkar (Canonical)
- **Focus**: AI-assisted development, language choices (TypeScript, Python, and Java), transitioning from prototypes to enterprise production systems, software craftsmanship, and architecture fundamentals.

### 3. Role of Technology – Yesterday, Today and Tomorrow
- **Speaker**: Mutheeshwaran S (Infosys)
- **Focus**: The evolution of technology paradigms, human impact, practical AI developments, and forward-looking engineering perspectives.

## Call for Proposals (CFP) Extended

The Call for Papers (CFP) deadline for speakers and project showcases has been extended:
- **Extended Deadline**: **November 6, 2026** (originally October 15, 2026)
- **Official Notice**: [DevCarnival CFP Update](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/dev-carnival_devcarnival2027-callforpapers-cfp-activity-7516372817399959553-MCvY)
