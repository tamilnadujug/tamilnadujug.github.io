## About DevCarnival 2027

**DevCarnival 2027** is a major community-driven developer conference taking place at the Infosys Campus in Mahindra City, Chennai, on January 8–9, 2027. Centered on the theme *"Technology. People. Purpose."*, the conference brings together developers, open-source contributors, technology leaders, and engineering students from across India.

Tamil Nadu Java User Group (TNJUG) is an official Community Partner for the conference.

## Conference Scope

- **Tracks**: 8+ parallel technology tracks spanning AI and developer productivity, cloud-native engineering, Java platforms, data architecture, and emerging systems.
- **Sessions**: 50+ technical talks and practical workshops.
- **Technology Pavilion**: 100+ interactive project demonstrations and exhibits.

## Confirmed Sessions

### 1. The AI Native Developer: What Programming Languages Will Matter Tomorrow?
- **Speaker**: Samir Kamerkar (Canonical)
- **Focus**: AI-assisted development, language choices (TypeScript, Python, and Java), transitioning from prototypes to enterprise production systems, software craftsmanship, and architecture fundamentals.

### 2. Role of Technology – Yesterday, Today and Tomorrow
- **Speaker**: Mutheeshwaran S (Infosys)
- **Focus**: The evolution of technology paradigms, human impact, practical AI developments, and forward-looking engineering perspectives.

## Community Collaboration

- **Role**: TNJUG is participating as an active Community Partner alongside regional technology user groups.
- **Call for Proposals (CFP)**: Open for community speakers through October 15, 2026.
