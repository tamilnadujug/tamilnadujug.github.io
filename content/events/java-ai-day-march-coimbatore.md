## About Java AI Day

**Java AI Day — March Edition** was a full-day, community-focused event organized by Tamilnadu JUG and hosted at EPAM Systems in Coimbatore, with support from **Bangalore Java User Group** in connecting speakers.

The gathering brought together engineers, architects, and open-source contributors for a full day of talks, discussions, and hands-on learning exploring the intersection of the Java ecosystem, open-source development, and artificial intelligence.


## Sessions & Presentations

### 1. Open Source & OpenJDK Contributions
- **Speaker**: Samir Kamerkar (Canonical)
- **Topic**: India's expanding role in global open-source software and concrete pathways for developers to contribute to foundational JVM projects such as OpenJDK.

### 2. Hands-on Spring AI Workshop
- **Speakers**: Muthukumaran Navaneethakrishnan (Software Engineer at KLA, author of *Spring AI for Your Organization*, creator of `mcp-server-bash-sdk`) & SHAAMA M
- **Topics**: Real-world Spring AI architectures, Large Language Model (LLM) integration, Model Context Protocol (MCP), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and cloud-native implementations.
- **Hands-on Format**: Interactive practical activities where attendees brought their own laptops to build and experiment directly.

### 3. Java's Evolution in the AI Era
- **Speaker**: Gireesh Punathil (IBM Master Inventor, Node.js Technical Steering Committee member with 20+ years building compilers, VMs, and platforms)
- **Topics**: The Java Enhancement Proposal (JEP) pipeline, upcoming JVM capabilities, and foundational enhancements supporting modern workloads.

### 4. From Copilot to Autonomous Agent: The Next Evolution of Software Engineering
- **Speaker**: Dinesh Kumar Ilangovan (Digital transformation leader with 20+ years experience)
- **Topics**: How developer tooling is evolving from interactive coding copilots to autonomous agents, and the architectural shifts required in software engineering.

## Community Activities & Initiatives

### "Java 25 in Practice" Quiz
The event featured an interactive community quiz titled **"Java 25 in Practice"**, testing knowledge of modern and upcoming Java features.

The event featured a dedicated book-distribution initiative. Technical books were selected and distributed to attendees based on community survey responses, including:

- *DSA in Practice*
