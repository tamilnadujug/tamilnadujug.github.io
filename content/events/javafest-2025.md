## About JAVAFEST’25

**JAVAFEST'25** was India's premier Java conference hosted at the expansive **Infosys Campus in Electronic City, Bangalore**.

To make the conference accessible to developers and engineering students across Tamil Nadu, **Tamil Nadu Java User Group (TNJUG)** organized a dedicated community bus delegation traveling directly from Coimbatore to Bangalore.

## Community Initiatives & Sponsorship

- **Bus Delegation**: Coordinated by Hari Nikesh R and Kishore G, enabling students and working developers to attend talks by global Java Champions and JVM engineers.
- **Academic Sponsorship**: **Sri Eshwar College of Engineering** joined as a proud sponsor of the initiative, demonstrating deep academic commitment to industry exposure.
- **Ink of Inspiration**: Collaborative community book and knowledge initiative shared among attendees.
