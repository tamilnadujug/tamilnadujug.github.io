## About the Panel Discussion

Hosted at **VET Institute of Arts and Science (Co-ed) College in Erode**, this collaborative panel discussion was organized alongside **TN Startup Thiruvizha** and **Nanuri Labs**.

The session explored how grassroots developer communities and hackathons serve as vital bridges connecting college campuses with Tamil Nadu's rapidly expanding startup and engineering ecosystem.

## Key Discussion Points

- **From College Projects to Real MVPs**: Transitioning beyond toy projects to production backend architectures.
- **Hackathons as Talent Launchpads**: Leveraging competitions to build portfolio evidence for top tech hiring.
- **Grassroots Mentorship**: How regional chapters empower tier-2 and tier-3 engineering students.
