## About the Roundtable

On August 25, 2026, **Tamil Nadu Java User Group (TNJUG)** hosted a special virtual roundtable with renowned speaker and author **Dr. Venkat Subramaniam**.

The discussion gathered college department heads, professors, engineering students, and experienced software architects to discuss modern pedagogical approaches to software development and actionable ways academic curricula can adapt to the modern software industry.

## Key Takeaways

- **Teaching Concepts over Syntax**: Why understanding underlying computational models matters far more than memorizing APIs.
- **Emphasizing Automated Testing**: Incorporating testing, code quality, and refactoring from the very first semester.
- **Open Source in the Classroom**: Encouraging students to read and contribute to open-source codebases early in their engineering journey.
