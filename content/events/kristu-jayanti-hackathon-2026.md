### Overview
The **Tamil Nadu Java User Group (TNJUG)** served as an organizer and project jury member at the Hackathon hosted at **Kristu Jayanti University – Airport Road Campus**, supporting campus builders and evaluating innovative engineering solutions.

### Key Highlights & Sessions
* **Developer Ecosystems Keynote:** Emphasized the vital role of open collaboration, community engagement, and peer-to-peer mentorship in driving engineering careers.
* **Spotlight on Devcarnival & JavaFest:** Introduced **[Devcarnival](https://devcarnival.com)** and **[JavaFest](https://javafest.org)** as ecosystem platforms for showcasing collegiate engineering talent.
* **Devcarnival Tech Pavilion Pipeline:** Evaluated and selected high-impact student projects to be presented and demonstrated at the upcoming Devcarnival Tech Pavilion.

### Acknowledgments & Coordination
* **Host Institution:** Sincere thanks to **Basavaraja Juttiyavar**, **Dr. Velmurugan R**, and the faculty and organizing team of **Kristu Jayanti College** for their hospitality and commitment to student innovation.
* **Community Coordinators:** Deep appreciation to **Hari Nikesh R**, **Rohan J**, **Geetha M**, and **Tejesh R** from **Tamil Nadu JUG** and **Bangalore Java User Group** for driving on-ground coordination and student mentoring.

> **Notice for Shortlisted Teams:** Please ensure your project confirmation form is completed to finalize your demonstration slot at the Devcarnival Tech Pavilion.
