## About the Kick-Off Discussion

Held on Teacher's Day (September 5, 2026) at **Ramakrishna Math in Chennai**, this collaborative gathering brought together volunteers, senior educators, and technology leaders to inaugurate organizing preparations for **DevCarnival 2027**.

The meeting centered on the guiding philosophy of making technology education, open-source contribution, and software engineering mentorship accessible to all students across Tamil Nadu.
