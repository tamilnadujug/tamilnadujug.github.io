## About the Chapter Launch

On October 2, 2026, the **Tamil Nadu Java User Group (TNJUG)** successfully hosted its first in-person meetup in Madurai and officially inaugurated the **TNJUG Madurai Chapter**.

Organized in collaboration with the **Tamil Nadu AI Association (TNAIA)**, the full-day gathering brought together developers, students, engineering leaders, and technology practitioners for deep-dive technical talks, hands-on engineering workshops, architectural discussions, and community networking.

## Confirmed Sessions & Workshops

### 1. JVM Performance at Scale
- **Speaker**: Vaibhav Choudhary (OpenJDK Author with 20+ years of JVM internals experience, currently at Salesforce; travelled from Bengaluru to Madurai)
- **Key Themes**: JVM internals, Just-In-Time (JIT) compilation, memory management, garbage collection mechanics, application scaling, and performance optimization in production environments.

### 2. Java Memory Management and Garbage Collection for Modern Applications
- **Speaker**: Pawan Chawdhary (Principal Engineer with 15 years of experience across Java engineering and cloud-native systems; travelled from Bengaluru to Madurai)
- **Key Themes**: JVM memory areas, GC fundamentals, comparative evaluation of collectors (Serial, Parallel, CMS, G1, and ZGC), balancing throughput vs. latency trade-offs, and measurement-driven GC decision-making.

### 3. Hands-on Workshop: Trust, but Verify — Building AI Agents Your Startup Can Actually Delegate To
- **Speaker**: Abdul Salam Mohamed Kani (Engineering Director with 20+ years in financial services technology)
- **Format**: 90-minute practical hands-on workshop on engineering reliable agentic workflows.
- **Key Themes**: Handling unstructured real-world inputs, deterministic action selection, tool invocation, structured outputs, verifying execution results before delegation, and analyzing failure modes and accountability.
