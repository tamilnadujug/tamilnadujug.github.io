### Overview

The **Green Lab** at Kongu Engineering College is an innovation and prototyping initiative established with the vision of **Sathish Kumar Thiyagarajan**, supported by **Dr. M. Geetha** and **Dr. PCD. Kalaivaani**, and guided by practical hardware expert **Ram Prakash**.

### Focus Areas

- **Hardware & Electronics**: Practical exposure to SMPS, motherboards, CPU components, system assembly, and hardware troubleshooting.
- **Embedded & Automotive Protocols**: Hands-on implementation of CAN (Controller Area Network) messages, DBC files, and Python/Java simulation tooling.
- **Modular JVM Systems**: Exploring JVM internals, bytecode, JNI native code integration, ProGuard optimization, and dynamic OSGi bundles on Apache Felix.
