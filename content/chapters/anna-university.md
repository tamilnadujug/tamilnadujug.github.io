### About CEG Anna University Chapter

Established in 2026, the College of Engineering Guindy (CEG), Anna University chapter is TNJUG's collegiate community in Chennai. CEG holds a distinguished legacy as one of India's oldest and most prestigious engineering institutions.

The TNJUG CEG chapter connects undergraduate and postgraduate students with industry practitioners, organizing technical sessions, hands-on workshops in modern Java, cloud architecture, and collaborative open-source projects.
