### About PSNA Chapter

The TNJUG chapter at PSNA College of Engineering and Technology in Dindigul brings together students across CSBS, CSE, IT, and AI departments to engage with enterprise Java, cloud engineering, and modern developer tooling.

Faculty leadership works in synergy with student organizers to host code clinics, Git sprints, and technical workshops.
