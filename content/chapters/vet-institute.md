### About the Chapter

The **VET Institute of Arts and Science (Co-education) College Chapter** in Erode brings modern enterprise Java, open-source culture, and student-led hackathons to the campus community. Through the School of Computing and Innovation Hub, students participate in hands-on workshops, peer coding bootcamps, and industry tech talks.
