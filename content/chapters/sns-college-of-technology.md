### About SNS College of Technology Chapter

Established in 2026, the **SNS College of Technology Chapter** of the Tamil Nadu Java User Group (TNJUG) represents our expanding collegiate developer community in Coimbatore, encompassing students and faculty across **SNS Institutions** (including SNS College of Technology and SNS College of Engineering).

SNS College of Technology is an autonomous engineering institution affiliated with Anna University, accredited with an **A++ Grade by NAAC** and recognized by NBA. Renowned as India's first institution to implement an institutionalized **Design Thinking (DT)** framework across higher education, SNSCT emphasizes experiential learning, agile innovation, and industry alignment through its specialized research centres, Design Thinking and Innovation Labs, and entrepreneurial incubation hubs.

### Community Initiatives & Focus Areas

The TNJUG chapter at SNS College of Technology collaborates closely with departmental leadership in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AIML), Computer Science, and Information Technology to deliver:

- **Enterprise Java & Cloud Backend Mastery:** Practical workshops on modern Java (Java 21/25), Spring Boot microservices, high-performance concurrency, and containerized cloud applications.
- **AI & Accelerated Computing:** Hands-on labs bridging the gap between enterprise Java architectures and applied artificial intelligence, including Spring AI, vector search, and NVIDIA GPU-accelerated computing pipelines.
- **Design Thinking in Software Engineering:** Empowering students to apply human-centered design principles (Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, Test) to software architecture, developer tooling, and open-source projects.
- **Collegiate Hackathons & Tech Sprints:** Hosting code clinics, open-source contribution drives, technical symposiums, and masterclasses led by seasoned industry architects and community leaders.
