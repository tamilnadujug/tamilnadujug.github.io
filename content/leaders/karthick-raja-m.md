### Chapter Leadership & Academic Role

Karthick Raja M is a **Campus Leader for the Sri Eshwar College of Engineering (SECE) Chapter** of TNJUG and serves as an **Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering** at SECE in Coimbatore. He also serves as the **GDG on Campus Faculty Mentor** at Sri Eshwar College of Engineering.

Dedicated to advancing technical culture and peer learning, Karthick actively mentors students in modern Java capabilities, conducts technical workshops, coordinates hackathons, and connects collegiate talent with real-world developer community ecosystems.
