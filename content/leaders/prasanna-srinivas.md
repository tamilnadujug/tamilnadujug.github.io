### Community Leadership & Role

Prasanna Srinivas is a **Core Member of the Tamil Nadu Java User Group (TNJUG)** and the **CEO & Founder of Skill Instill Technologies**. With a deep commitment to talent acceleration and technical enablement, Prasanna plays an active role in community building, engineering skill development, and fostering industry-academia synergy across Tamil Nadu.

Through his leadership at Skill Instill Technologies and his collaboration with TNJUG, he advocates for hands-on technical training, bridging the gap between collegiate education and enterprise engineering standards, and empowering developers and student cohorts with industry-ready skills.

- **Talent Enablement & EdTech:** Enterprise training, technology hiring, upskilling student cohorts, and campus-to-corporate transitions.
- **Community & Ecosystem Building:** Fostering technical meetups, developer forums, and open learning platforms across Tamil Nadu.
- **Technology Solutions & Consulting:** IT consulting, talent transformation, and technical program management.
