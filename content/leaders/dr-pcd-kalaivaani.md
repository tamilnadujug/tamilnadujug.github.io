### Chapter Leadership & Academic Role

Dr. PCD. Kalaivaani is a **Professional Chapter Leader for the Kongu Engineering College (KEC) Chapter** of TNJUG and serves as a **Faculty member in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering** at Kongu Engineering College in Perundurai. She also serves as the **Coordinator of the Cloud Users Group** at KEC.

An active academic mentor and volunteer for tech talks, student sessions, and campus events, Dr. Kalaivaani is dedicated to creating enriching opportunities for students and peers to learn emerging technologies, exchange ideas, and build practical software engineering and cloud capabilities.
