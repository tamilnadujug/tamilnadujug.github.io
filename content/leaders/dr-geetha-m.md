### Community Leadership & Role

Dr. Geetha M is the **Head of Chapters for the Tamil Nadu Java User Group (TNJUG)** and an **Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Kongu Engineering College (KEC)**. She coordinates the liaison between higher educational institutions and TNJUG, guiding collegiate chapter formations and faculty alignments across Tamil Nadu.
