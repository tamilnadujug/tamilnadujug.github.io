### Student Leadership & Campus Role

Mithun R is a **Campus Leader for the VET Institute of Arts and Science (VET IAS) Chapter** of the Tamil Nadu Java User Group (TNJUG). Currently in his 3rd year pursuing B.Sc. in Computer Science, he serves as the **Secretary in the School of Computing** at VET IAS in Erode.

An enthusiastic student community organizer, Mithun actively drives campus hackathons, technical sessions, coding workshops, and panel discussions. Passionate about peer learning and Java software development, he focuses on connecting students with industry practices, encouraging open-source participation, and building a thriving student developer culture across campus.
