### Chapter Leadership & Academic Role

Dr. P. Shanthi is a **Chapter Leader for the College of Engineering Guindy, Anna University Chapter** of TNJUG and serves as an **Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering** at CEG, Anna University in Chennai.

With extensive experience in academic instruction and technical mentorship—including conducting advanced Java programming courses, tech talks, hackathons, and student sessions—Dr. Shanthi actively fosters developer collaboration and student engagement in enterprise Java and modern software engineering practices.
