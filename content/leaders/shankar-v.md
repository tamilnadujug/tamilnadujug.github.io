### Student Leadership & Campus Role

Shankar V is a **Campus Leader for the Sri Eshwar College of Engineering (SECE) Chapter** of the Tamil Nadu Java User Group (TNJUG). Currently in his 3rd year pursuing Computer Science and Engineering at SECE in Coimbatore, Shankar also serves as a **Campus Ambassador for GeeksforGeeks**.

An active hackathon participant, community builder, and technical organizer, Shankar is dedicated to bringing TNJUG’s developer ecosystem closer to college students. He leads campus initiatives including technical sessions, hands-on Java workshops, coding activities, hackathons, and open-source project collaborations—fostering an active and welcoming community where students learn by building and connect with the broader developer ecosystem.
