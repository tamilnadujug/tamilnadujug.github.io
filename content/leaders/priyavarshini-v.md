### Student Leadership & Campus Role

Priyavarshini V is a **Campus Leader for the Sri Eshwar College of Engineering (SECE) Chapter** of the Tamil Nadu Java User Group (TNJUG). Currently in her 3rd year pursuing B.E. in Computer Science and Engineering (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning) at SECE in Coimbatore, she is also an **SDE Intern at FQCI (NQM IISc CDAC, Bengaluru)**.

With extensive community leadership experience as a **Google Gemini Student Ambassador**, **Ex-Adobe Student Campus Ambassador**, and **RGKUT IBM Qiskit Mentor**, Priyavarshini brings strong expertise in community engagement, student awareness sessions, and event organization. At SECE, she spearheads TNJUG campus initiatives including hands-on Java workshops, coding challenges, peer-learning circles, and beginner-friendly project activities—empowering students to gain practical engineering experience and connect with the broader Java developer ecosystem.
