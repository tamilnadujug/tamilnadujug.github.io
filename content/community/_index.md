### Welcome to Tamil Nadu Java User Group (TNJUG)

TNJUG is an independent, non-profit developer community established to champion modern Java technologies, JVM research, cloud native architectures, and open source collaboration across Tamil Nadu, India.

Our community brings together:
- **Practicing Engineers & Architects**: Senior technologists shaping enterprise backends who want to share practical field experience and explore emerging JVM specifications.
- **Engineering Students**: Aspiring software engineers looking for mentorship, hands-on workshops, open source contribution avenues, and industry best practices.
- **Academic Champions & Faculty**: Professors and department heads fostering deep computer science rigor and bridging the curriculum-to-industry gap.

### Community Code of Conduct

TNJUG is dedicated to providing a harassment-free, welcoming, and safe learning environment for everyone, regardless of gender, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, race, ethnicity, religion, or technical experience level.

#### Our Standards
- **Be Respectful & Constructive**: Disagreements happen, but personal attacks or dismissive attitudes towards beginners are strictly prohibited.
- **Share Knowledge Generously**: Welcome questions from learners and junior colleagues.
- **Respect Commercial Boundaries**: TNJUG is a technical, educational forum. Direct vendor pitches, unsolicited recruitment spam, or product marketing without educational substance are not allowed during meetups.

To report any code of conduct violation, please email **[conduct@tamilnadujug.org](mailto:contact@tamilnadujug.org)**.

### How to Start or Host a Chapter

Are you interested in launching a TNJUG chapter at your university, or hosting an upcoming meetup at your company campus?

#### Minimum Requirements for Campus Chapters:
1. One designated **Faculty Advisor** (Department Head or Senior Faculty).
2. At least two active **Student Chapter Leads**.
3. Dedicated lab space or seminar hall with projector and internet connectivity.
4. Commitment to host at least two technical meetups or hands-on labs per academic semester.

Reach out to our organizing committee at **[contact@tamilnadujug.org](mailto:contact@tamilnadujug.org)** to begin the chapter onboarding process.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Is TNJUG free to join?
Yes! TNJUG is 100% free and open to everyone. Meetups and workshops are free of charge.

#### Do I need to be an expert to attend?
No. Our sessions cater to both curious beginners and seasoned principal engineers.
