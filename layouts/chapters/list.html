How TNJUG Chapters Operate

We combine academic rigour with modern JVM practices used by production teams.

01.

Faculty Leadership

Department heads and senior professors mentor chapter initiatives and integrate practical modern Java topics into departmental labs.

02.

Student Organizers

Passionate student developers host peer coding sessions, hackathons, and coordinate logistics with state-wide TNJUG organizers.

03.

Industry Mentors

Architects and senior engineers visit campus or dial in to conduct JVM performance clinics, Spring AI labs, and career guidance.
