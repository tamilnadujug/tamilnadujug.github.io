Open Source Initiatives & The Open Source Lab

Connecting academic learning with production industry software engineering through real-world open-source contributions, hands-on internships, hardware prototyping, and community leadership across Tamil Nadu.

Flagship Initiative

The Open Source Lab at Kongu Engineering College

Established as an academic-industry partnership in collaboration with the Tamil Nadu Java User Group (TNJUG) and Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Kongu Engineering College, the Open Source Lab provides students with a transformative software engineering environment.

Initiated by Rohan J and guided by dedicated industry architects and faculty leaders, students build production-grade open-source platforms, intern at cutting-edge tech firms, engage with district administration, and mentor peer developers across the state.

The 6-Phase Learning Roadmap

A progressive software engineering pathway combining intensive technical bootcamps, architectural team structures, remote open-source workflows, and real-world internships.

Impact & Outreach

Field Milestones & Community Engagement

From presentations to District Collectors to live conference talks, the Open Source Lab directly connects student engineers with governance and industry.

100 Schools Expansion

Ramanathapuram District Collector

Presented TNEBooks to Thiru. M. Sivaguru Prabhakaran, I.A.S., securing official permission to deploy digital educational materials across 50 government and 50 aided schools.

Industry Speakers

EPAM Systems Java AI Meetup

Student developers Keerthana Rajendran and Sindhu Dhanashekaran presented the SQLBuilder open-source project to industry engineers, earning the Book of Appreciation.

Governance & TOSS

HackFusion & Erode Collector

Demonstrated TNEBooks to Thiru. S. Kandasamy, IAS (Erode District Collector) at VETIAS, and showcased the initiative to Samir Kamerkar (Engineering Director at Canonical) at TOSS 2026.

Field Validation

Grassroots Classroom Outreach

Fieldwork at Government Model School, Thinaikulam Govt School, and Kongu Matric HSS (Uthukuli), gathering feedback directly from educators and mentoring high-school students.

Hardware & IoT
